# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Beverage store customers. They use this in a fast-paced environment (at the store counter), often one-handed or while holding other items. They need the interaction to be extremely quick and clear.

## Product Purpose
Allow customers to borrow eco-cups via QR code scanning, track their current borrowed cups (with return deadlines), and view their usage history and rewards. Success means a user can borrow a cup in under 5 seconds.

## Brand Personality
Functional, fast, clear, and reliable. The design should get out of the user's way and focus purely on utility.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered marketing pages with heavy graphics
- Interfaces with tiny text or low contrast (hard to read outdoors)
- Complex navigation trees (everything should be 1-2 taps away)

## Design Principles
- Optimize for speed: Fast loading, immediate scanning readiness.
- High contrast: Must be readable in sunlight and various lighting conditions.
- Clear state: It must be instantly obvious if a cup is borrowed, returned, or overdue.
- Mobile-first utility: Large tap targets, bottom-heavy navigation for thumb reach.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast ratios (WCAG AA at minimum). Large, distinct tap targets for one-handed use.
